# Cashier Guide — Wholesale POS

## Starting Your Shift

1. Tap the **Register** tab.
2. If no shift is open, you'll see **Open Shift**. Enter your starting cash count (the amount of money physically in the drawer) and tap **Start**.
3. A live timer and running totals appear for the duration of your shift.

> **Tip:** You can open a shift even if the previous one hasn't been closed — the system will auto-close the prior shift with a discrepancy warning.

## Processing a Sale

1. Tap **New Sale** (or press `F2`).
2. Search or scan products. Add quantities; the price shown is your negotiated wholesale rate.
3. Apply discounts if authorized (percentage or fixed-amount). Discounts above your threshold require manager approval.
4. Review the cart, then tap **Complete Sale**.
5. Choose payment method (cash, mobile money, bank transfer) and enter amount tendered. Change is calculated automatically.
6. Print or email the receipt. A copy is saved to **History → Today**.

## Voiding a Sale

You **cannot** void a sale yourself. Instead:

1. Find the sale in History.
2. Tap **Request Void** and enter a reason.
3. An Owner receives a notification and either approves or rejects.
4. If approved, the sale status changes to `VOIDED` and the shift reconciliation reflects the reversal.

## Ending Your Shift

1. Return to the **Register** tab.
2. Enter the physical cash count in the drawer.
3. The app computes expected vs actual and highlights any variance.
4. Tap **Close Shift**. A summary receipt prints; a duplicate saves to the report log.

> **Cash movement logging:** Before closing, record any cash removed from the drawer (deposit to bank, personal withdrawal) using **Add Movement**. These are subtracted from your closing calculation so variance reflects only true discrepancies.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| `F2` | New sale |
| `Esc` | Clear search / go back |
| `F9` | Quick-search product (type then press F9) |
| `Ctrl+F` | Focus search field |

## What You Cannot Do

- View or edit finance entries.
- Access warehouse/picklist screens.
- See other cashiers' transactions outside your own shift.
- Modify user accounts or settings.

These restrictions are enforced client-side and server-side.
